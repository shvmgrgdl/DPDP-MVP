## Helping owners follow along
- The **welcome** appears on first open (and after a reset). Walk through its 3 steps: parents choose → every photo is checked → proof for everything.
- On any screen, tap **About this page** (top bar) for a plain-words explanation.
- For owners who ask “what does the law actually require?”, open **DPDP in 2 minutes** (sidebar).

## Guardrails (say it this way)
- Say "DPDP readiness / managed programme / coverage". Never "100% compliant" or "certified".
- **Privacy contact ≠ statutory DPO.** A DPO, independent auditor and annual DPIA are required only if the school is notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary.
- School exemptions (Fourth Schedule) cover educational and safety tracking and bus location. They do **not** cover marketing, media or ads.
- Face matching is used only to enforce parents' choices. Unknown faces are never guessed.
- The assistant never gives final legal determinations; it escalates to counsel.
- Full DPDP Rules obligations apply from 13 May 2027 (rules notified 13 Nov 2025). Every personal-data breach is reported to parents and the Board; the detailed Board report is due within 72 hours.

## Before every demo
- Reset demo data, set the school name and logo, and open each video once.
- Use Chrome at 1440×900 or larger; the parent app renders in a phone frame.
- Offline fallback: `npm run demo` on the laptop.
