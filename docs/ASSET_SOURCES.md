# Media Asset Sources

Stock photos/videos of Indian/South Asian school children sourced from Pexels (free to use, no
attribution legally required, but credited below anyway). All photos vetted for visible faces
and school context; low-res previews were checked before downloading full-res (w=1600) files.

`series tag` groups shots believed to be from the same shoot/photographer batch — useful for the
"this child appears in N photos" recurring-child demo. The **BIPS Patiala** series (16 photos) is
the strongest for that: same kids, same red school uniform, across classroom/science-fair/sports-day.

required — https://www.pexels.com/license/).

## What failed / was rejected

- Pexels/Unsplash/Pixabay search APIs and pexels.com direct HTML (curl) are blocked on this
  network; WebFetch on pexels.com URLs worked reliably and was used for all discovery instead of
  WebSearch snippets alone.
- No dedicated "school annual-day stage performance" series was found (real school-stage dance
  photos on Pexels are rare); substituted a genuine Meghalaya cultural-festival children's-costume
  series (Euwan Marbaniang) — flagged above, swap out if strict "school stage" framing matters.
- Rejected on sight: Vietnamese/Indonesian/Turkish/Latin American children mislabeled by search as
  "Indian" (e.g. vietchung award-ceremony series, ng-c-bich-kieu tug-of-war series, Tkirkgoz series,
  cottonbro/Katerina Holmes/RDNE/Tima Miroshnichenko generic global stock), adult-only dance
  performers (Rajasthani fire-pot dancers), faces-not-visible shots (aerial assemblies, backs of
  heads, silhouettes), and a 4K video (15957335, Independence Day flag scene) that came in at 28.8MB
  — over the 25MB cap with no smaller variant discoverable — dropped in favour of
  school-ceremony-assembly.mp4.
- Did not find authentic Indian science-fair "volcano model" / "solar system project" photos —
  all matching search results were Western home-schooling stock (RDNE, Vanessa Loring); left out
  rather than mis-tag as Indian.
